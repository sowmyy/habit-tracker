Privacy Policy

HabiTracker · Last updated: August 26, 2026

HabiTracker is a personal habit-tracking web app. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used. In short: the app has no server and collects nothing for the developer — your data stays in your own Google account.

Information the app accesses

  • Basic Google profile (name, email address, and profile picture) — used only to sign you in and show your name/avatar inside the app.
  • A single Google Sheet in your Google Drive — the app creates one spreadsheet named Habit Tracker Data in your Drive and reads and writes only that file to store your habit list and daily check-ins. It uses the drive.file permission, which grants access only to files the app itself creates. The app cannot see any of your other Drive files.

How your data is stored and used

  • Your habits and progress live entirely in that Google Sheet, in your Drive.
  • There is no application server or database operated by the developer. Your data is never sent to, collected by, or stored by the developer or any third party.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.
  • The app communicates only with Google's own APIs (Sign-In, Drive, and Sheets) to provide these features, and loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Data retention and deletion

You are always in control of your data. To remove it:

  • Delete the Habit Tracker Data spreadsheet from your Google Drive, and/or
  • Revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
